# THE FOUR GATES roadmap

**Overall:** roughly **800 hours** of work to a full open-world game (Founder, 2 Nov 2026): the city and its four
gates, the regions beyond them, quests, characters, the economy, learning threads, art, sound and release. The table
below is the first playable slice only.

THE FOUR GATES started on 2 Nov 2026. These are estimates for the first playable slice, not approvals: each item is
designed and locked by the Founder before it is built.

| Work | Estimated hours | Intended result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Game design foundations | 10 | Core loop (tasks, earning, exploring, learning), the player's goal, the first area's map and the art direction, agreed and locked. |
| Godot project and tooling | 4 | Empty Godot 4 project, folder layout, input map, a test scene and a repeatable way to run and check it. |
| Player and camera | 10 | A third-person character who walks, runs, climbs steps and interacts, with a smooth camera. |
| First district | 16 | One explorable market quarter with streets, a river edge, shops and day and night lighting (lanterns at night). |
| Tasks and money | 12 | A task board and three starter tasks that pay coins, with a simple wallet and shop. |
| Characters and dialogue | 10 | Talkable townspeople, a dialogue system and choices that change how people treat you. |
| Learning thread | 8 | A first scholar's errand that teaches something about the period through play. |
| Save, menus and polish | 8 | Save and load, a pause menu, settings, and a playable build for Mac. |
| Documentation and tests | 4 | Setup guide, credits, and automated checks for the scripts. |

Source of this plan: the Founder's direction in `GAME/OVERVIEW/Summary.md` and the build queue in
`GAME/APPROVED/BUILD_QUEUE.md`.
