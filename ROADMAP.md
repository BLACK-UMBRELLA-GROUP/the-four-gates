# THE FOUR GATES roadmap

**Overall:** roughly **800 hours** of work to a full open-world game (Founder, 2 Nov 2026): the city and its four
gates, the regions beyond them, quests, characters, the economy, learning threads, art, sound and release. The first nine
rows are the first playable slice; the rows after them were designed and locked on 9 Nov 2026 and are not yet built
or estimated.

THE FOUR GATES started on 2 Nov 2026. These are estimates for the first playable slice, not approvals: each item is
designed and locked by the Founder before it is built.

| Work | Estimated hours | Intended result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Game design foundations | 10 | Core loop (tasks, earning, exploring, learning), the player's goal, the first area's map and the art direction, agreed and locked. Done 2026-11-06 (LOCK 1). |
| Godot project and tooling | 4 | Empty Godot 4 project, folder layout, input map, a test scene and a repeatable way to run and check it. Done 2026-11-06 (LOCK 1). |
| Player and camera | 10 | A third-person character who walks, runs, climbs steps and interacts, with a smooth camera. Done 2026-11-06 (LOCK 1). |
| First district | 16 | One explorable market quarter with streets, a river edge, shops and day and night lighting (lanterns at night). |
| Tasks and money | 12 | A task board and three starter tasks that pay coins, with a simple wallet and shop. |
| Characters and dialogue | 10 | Talkable townspeople, a dialogue system and choices that change how people treat you. |
| Learning thread | 8 | A first scholar's errand that teaches something about the period through play. Done 2026-11-11 (LOCK 3 part 1). |
| Save, menus and polish | 8 | Save and load, a pause menu, settings, and a playable build for Mac (LOCKs 29, 101). |
| Documentation and tests | 4 | Setup guide, credits, and automated checks for the scripts. |
| Clothes, inventory and owning things | not estimated | Clothes and a bigger bag, then animals, bees, a house and orchard, a stall and a boat, giving for good, and family (LOCKs 5, 88-94). Designed and locked, not built. |
| Temptations and fair dealing | not estimated | The sealed purse, the dice house, Qasim's jars, Rashid's loan and partnership (LOCKs 10-12, 16, 31). Designed and locked, not built. |
| Prayer, the calendar and giving | not estimated | The prayer record, a real lunar calendar with rest days, the fasting month, both Eids, zakat, feeding people, pilgrims you send, Qurans and school (LOCKs 8-9, 18-25, 30, 74-87). Designed and locked, not built. |
| The world beyond River Market | not estimated | Through the Gate of Trust to Jisra, the road map and gates that shut at night, and the end of chapter 1 (LOCKs 32-39). Designed and locked, not built. |
| Chapters 2-4 and the finale | not estimated | Seven more towns, three chapters and the ending (LOCKs 40-51). Designed and locked, not built. |
| Side missions | not estimated | Twelve River Market side missions (LOCKs 52-63). Designed and locked, not built. |
| The scholar's notebook and collectibles | not estimated | Notebook pages, the Names learned, fountains and stars, and 50 masjids to find (LOCKs 15, 26, 33, 64-73, 95-97, 102). Designed and locked, not built. |

Source of this plan: the Founder's direction in `GAME/OVERVIEW/Summary.md`, the LOCK lists in
